Purpose

Siedler Österreich is a non-commercial browser game in alpha stage. It serves the playful exploration of public Austrian cadastre data, raises awareness for nature conservation and biodiversity, and demonstrates data processing methods.

Source code

The source code of this project is openly available on GitHub.

Data sources and licences

The map display is based on open data. Every source is fetched server-side; the browser never talks to the data providers. A machine-readable list with fetch location, licence, attribution and maximum cache age per source is available at /lizenzen and /api/licenses.

  • Cadastre (parcels, building footprints, land-use areas): © BEV – Federal Office of Metrology and Surveying, cadastral map as vector tiles from kataster.bev.gv.at, licensed CC BY 4.0. Mandatory notice: “© BEV, 2026 – Datenquelle: Bundesamt für Eich- und Vermessungswesen, Kataster (CC BY 4.0), bearbeitet”. The tiles are assembled live for the visible 0.02° grid cell by bevdirect-serve on our server (merged, coordinates rounded, area per land-use class measured) and cached for at most 24 hours. We keep no cadastre database and do not use the Katasterservice (JSON API); nothing can be queried by parcel number, folio (EZ) or owner. BEV does not endorse this project.
  • Administrative boundaries (KG and municipality register): BEV OGD Verwaltungsgrenzen (VGD) 1:50 000, reference date 2026-04-01, CC BY 4.0, modified (embedded table).
  • Terrain, land cover, tree and building heights, relief: derived from the BEV ALS elevation model (DTM/DSM 1 m) and orthophoto (CC BY 4.0, modified), Copernicus Sentinel-1/-2 (ESA), ESA WorldCover (CC BY 4.0), Hansen/UMD Global Forest Change (CC BY 4.0) – served through the public tier of srtm-lidar-at (bbox, point and KG-code queries only, no parcel data), 25 m grid, averaged per parcel; cached at most 24 h.
  • Context data (point and bbox queries only, no cadastre) through the public tier of umfeld-at, cached at most 24 h: Municipalities and land-price model: Source STATISTIK AUSTRIA (CC BY 4.0) – model values, no offers. Protected areas: Natura 2000, European Environment Agency (EEA); WDPA (UNEP-WCMC & IUCN, non-commercial). Roads, water, rail, transit stops, addresses: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 (OSM-derived fields are kept separate). Field and place names: BEV – Geographic Names (DLM), CC BY 4.0, modified. Legal references: RIS, Federal Chancellery (CC BY 4.0).
  • Field blocks (INVEKOS Schläge) and farmsteads: AMA / BML, INSPIRE INVEKOS via data.gv.at, CC BY 4.0 – aggregated, farmsteads as hashed points without names. Subsidy profiles (municipal aggregates only, no recipient data): AMA Transparenzdatenbank, CC BY 4.0.
  • Forest loss and carbon flux: Hansen et al. Global Forest Change 2024 v1.12 and Harris et al. Forest Carbon Flux (Global Forest Watch), CC BY 4.0. Timber harvest and prices: Statistics Austria (CC BY 4.0), LK timber market reports (preise.agrarforschung.at) – model values, no offers.
  • Groundwater, gauges, monitoring stations, nitrate, water protection areas: BML eHYD, Wasserschatz Österreich 2021, WISE / EEA, CC BY 4.0. Drought index: Copernicus European Drought Observatory. Water flow path: MERIT Hydro / MERIT-Basins via mghydro.com, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (display only, non-commercial).
  • National border: geoBoundaries gbOpen, CC BY-SA 4.0 (simplified). Red List: IUCN European Red List / EEA.
